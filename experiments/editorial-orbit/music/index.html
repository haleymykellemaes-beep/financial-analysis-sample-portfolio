How I built it

The Music app exports a library as one XML file. A short Python script loads it into a single SQLite table, tracks, keeping the columns the questions need: song, artist, genre, release year, length, play and skip counts, and dates. File paths, Apple’s internal IDs and playlist names stay out.

Apple tags songs with more than 100 genre labels, including duplicates such as “Hip-Hop/Rap”, “Hip Hop/Rap”, “Hip-Hop” and “Rap”. A view groups them into seven families before any question is asked.

What the numbers are, and aren’t. Play and skip counts are Apple’s running totals for songs in my library, as of the export on . Songs I streamed without saving aren’t here; Replay covers those. “Hours” multiplies plays by song length, so it assumes every play ran to the end.
