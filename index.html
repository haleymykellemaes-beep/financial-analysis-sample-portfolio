Approach

A number is useful once it can support a decision.

I am a data analyst interested in financial modeling, probability, risk, revenue intelligence, and the systems that make those questions easier to see.

The work starts with the decision, then the record behind it: what was booked, what is still open, what a price implies, and which assumption would change the answer. Excel, SQL, and Python are the tools. Clarity is the standard.

This site is a visual prototype for that practice. It is separate from my data analytics portfolio, and the interfaces below are proposed systems, not completed studies.
